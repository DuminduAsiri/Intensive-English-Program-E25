The World 200 Years From Now

It was the year 2226. The world was very different from today. Cities were full of tall buildings, flying cars, and smart robots. People could travel from one country to another in only a few minutes.

One morning, a young boy named Adam woke up and looked outside his window. He saw flying buses moving between the buildings. Robots were cleaning the streets, and special machines were planting trees.

Technology had also changed the way people lived. Houses could talk to people and prepare food automatically. Doctors used advanced machines to find and treat diseases. People could even visit space for holidays.

However, the people of 2226 had learned an important lesson. They knew that technology alone could not make the world happy. They protected nature, saved water, and used clean energy.

Adam smiled as he looked at the green city. He thought, "The future is amazing, but we must take care of our planet."

The world 200 years from now was not perfect, but people were working together to make it a better and happier place.
