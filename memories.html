Learning, Growing, and Succeeding Together.

Our journey

From whiteboard to launch

01

The idea

Our English program work was scattered across chat groups and drives. The web committee proposed one shared home for every assignment, presentation and recording.

02

Planning the pages

We sketched the site on a whiteboard — home, about, activities, groups — and agreed that every group should manage its own members and uploads.

03

Building it

Late evenings of layout work, blue theme experiments inspired by the faculty site, and a secure private portal for the four accounts.

04

Reviews and fixes

We demoed the site to the batch and the teachers' panel, collected feedback and refined the navigation, filters and file uploads.

05

Launch day

The archive went live for all four groups — and it keeps growing with every new submission we publish.
