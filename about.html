About us

The people behind the English language program

01-04 Group CD is the English language program team of the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya. Our work is guided by the teachers' panel of English and organised by the web committee.

Who we are

Six student groups working through assignments, presentations and collaborative activities as part of the faculty English language program.

What we do

We document every submission — reports, slide decks, recordings and reflections — so the whole batch can learn from each other.

Why this site

One reliable archive of our English program work, maintained by the web committee and reviewed by the teachers' panel.

Our commitment

Communication skills for engineers

The English language program prepares us to write clear technical reports, present design work with confidence and take part in professional discussions. Every group carries that responsibility for its own members, and this archive keeps the standard visible to everyone who comes after us.
