How we work

Learning English as engineers, together

The program pairs language practice with the kind of communication engineers actually need: technical reports, design presentations, meeting discussions and professional correspondence. Each group works through the same activities and then shares its results here.

Academic writing

Report structure, referencing, summarising technical material and editing our own drafts.

Speaking & presenting

Delivering technical talks with clear pronunciation, pacing and confident body language.

Discussion & debate

Group discussions, interviews and role plays that build fluency under pressure.

Reading & research

Working through journals, standards and articles to gather evidence for our work.
